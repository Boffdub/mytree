My Tree Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

My Tree ("the app," "we," "us") is a climate education quiz app. This page explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Brett Duboff, who runs My Tree. For any question about this policy or your data, contact bduboff@gmail.com.

What we collect

Using My Tree requires an account, created through Google, Apple, or email sign-in. We collect and store:

  • Account details: your email address, and your first and last name and profile picture if you add them yourself or sign in with Google or Apple and choose to share them
  • Quiz activity: which questions you've answered, whether you got them right, your difficulty setting, and your scores over time
  • Feedback: any message you send through the Contact Us screen, along with your account email
  • Sign-in session: a token that keeps you signed in on your device

Signing in with Google or Apple: that provider handles the sign-in according to its own privacy policy. We only receive the basic profile information needed to create your account.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard shows other players your display name (your first and last name, or "Anonymous" if you haven't added one), your rank, your number of correct answers, and your accuracy. Other players can see these values. Your email address and profile picture are never shown on the leaderboard.

Why we collect it and our legal basis

  • To create and run your account and track your progress: needed to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • To power the leaderboard and statistics: needed to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • To respond to feedback you send us: our legitimate interest in fixing problems and improving the app.
  • To send sign-in emails: needed to provide the service you asked for (contract).

Who processes your data

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, account sign-in, and profile picture storage. Data is stored in the United States (Supabase region us-east-1).
  • Google and Apple: sign-in, if you choose to use them.
  • Resend: sends sign-in emails and feedback notifications.

Because these providers store data in the United States, your data is transferred outside the European Economic Area. Transfers are covered by the data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses that these providers offer. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising or marketing.

Automated decisions

We don't use profiling or automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app. This removes your profile (including any uploaded profile picture), your quiz history, and your sign-in account. Copies in backups may remain for a limited period after deletion.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct data that is wrong (you can edit your name and picture in the app)
  • delete your data (Settings → Delete Account)
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • restrict or object to how we use your data

To exercise any of these, email bduboff@gmail.com. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Children's privacy

My Tree is not intended for anyone under 16, or under the higher minimum age in your country. We don't knowingly collect information from children below that age. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change to this policy, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, not just by updating the date at the top of this page.
